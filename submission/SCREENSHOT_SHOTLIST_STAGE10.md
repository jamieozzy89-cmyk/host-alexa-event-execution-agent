# Stage 10 — Screenshot / Gallery Shot List

Goal: a small set of screenshots that immediately communicates **execution**, not generic chat.

Use the verified Stage 09 product state. Do not stage fake data that the application cannot produce.

## Shot 1 — Hero / event created

**View:** Plan

**State:** Six-person dinner created with visible time, budget and guest constraints.

**Purpose:** Establish the product and visual quality quickly.

**Caption:** “From a natural hosting request to authoritative event state.”

## Shot 2 — Menu choice + confirmation

**View:** Plan

**State:** Three compatible menu options visible, with a selected option waiting for confirmation.

**Purpose:** Show structured Alexa+-style choice and the proposal-versus-commitment distinction.

**Caption:** “Suggestions stay suggestions until the host confirms them.”

## Shot 3 — Shopping execution

**View:** Plan

**State:** Shopping list with required / on-hand / to-buy quantities.

**Purpose:** Prove Host is an execution system rather than a conversational list generator.

**Caption:** “Shopping deficits are calculated from confirmed inventory.”

## Shot 4 — Hands-busy Live Mode

**View:** Live

**State:** One ready task with large controls and visible Next guidance.

**Purpose:** Strongest usability shot; should work as a gallery thumbnail candidate.

**Caption:** “Live Mode keeps the host focused on the current authoritative next task.”

## Shot 5 — Late seventh vegan guest

**View:** Plan / change review

**State:** Change-impact card visible before confirmation.

**Purpose:** Demonstrate the core differentiator.

**Caption:** “A late change is analysed before Host changes the plan.”

## Shot 6 — Activity receipts

**View:** Activity

**State:** Successful/failed/reversed receipt history visible.

**Purpose:** Show the execution-integrity principle visually.

**Caption:** “Receipts show what actually happened — not what the conversation claimed.”

## Shot 7 — Voice state

**View:** Live or Plan

**State:** Voice listening/processing/speaking indicator plus visible current task/result.

**Purpose:** Show multimodal interaction without making voice the entire product story.

**Caption:** “Voice and touch share the same controlled execution path.”

## Shot 8 — Mobile fallback

**Viewport:** 390×844

**State:** A useful Plan or Live state with controls visible.

**Purpose:** Prove responsive design and touch parity beyond the Echo Show-like layout.

**Caption:** “The same execution journey remains usable on mobile-sized touch surfaces.”

## Capture rules

- Prefer 1280×800 for the main Alexa-style gallery assets.
- Include at least one mobile shot but do not make mobile the hero image.
- Use the app's natural light/dark theme only; no external mockup frame that could imply physical Alexa certification.
- Keep browser chrome out of final crops where possible.
- Never crop away the “simulation” disclosure when a screenshot could otherwise imply a real checkout/device integration.
- Use actual application-generated state.
- Keep text legible at Devpost gallery size.
- Avoid terminal screenshots except possibly one final technical-evidence image if the gallery has room after product shots.
