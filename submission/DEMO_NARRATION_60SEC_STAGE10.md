# Stage 10 — 60-second Public Demo Narration Guide

This narration is designed for the verified real-app visual source slowed to approximately 60 seconds.

Visual candidate generated from the uninterrupted Host source run:

- duration: **59.986 seconds**;
- source behavior: real working application, no invented product states;
- narration target: approximately 125 words/minute;
- music: none required.

## Narration

### 0:00–0:07 — Integrity hook

**Host is an Alexa+ event-execution simulation built around one rule: an assistant should never claim work happened just because it said so.**

### 0:07–0:17 — Event and menu

**I start with a natural dinner request. Host turns it into authoritative event state, proposes compatible menus, and waits for confirmation before committing anything.**

### 0:17–0:27 — Shopping / simulation boundary

**Shopping is calculated from required quantities minus confirmed inventory. Product choices and checkout are clearly simulation-only.**

### 0:27–0:35 — Live Mode

**In Live Mode, Host surfaces one current ready task at a time.**

### 0:35–0:50 — Late-change differentiator

**Now the plan changes: a seventh guest is added, and they’re vegan. Host analyses the impact first, shows what would change, and preserves unaffected completed work. Only after confirmation does it replan.**

### 0:50–0:55 — Receipts

**Finally, Activity receipts show what actually succeeded.**

### 0:55–1:00 — Multimodal close

**Touch, keyboard and browser voice all use this same controlled execution path.**

## Recording controls

- Use a natural human voice; do not use the robotic local `espeak` placeholder.
- Keep the read conversational rather than rushing to match exact second marks.
- Do not add background music unless its rights are unquestionably cleared; no music is needed.
- If voice is mentioned, do not imply the automated deterministic speech fakes used in browser tests are live microphone proof.
- Keep the on-screen `Alexa+ simulation` label visible.
- Do not describe demo products/checkout as real purchasing.
- Final public upload must remain under three minutes, public on YouTube or Vimeo, and in English.

## Recommended final cut

The 60-second candidate is intentionally much shorter than the three-minute maximum. It leads with the execution-integrity idea, shows the complete differentiating workflow, and leaves little room for judges to miss the late-change/receipt story.

If the human narration needs a few additional seconds, extend only with short holds on Live Mode, the late-change impact card or Activity receipts. Do not add invented UI or unrelated architecture slides merely to use more of the three-minute allowance.
