## Visual-audit control

The capture workflow proves that the images correspond to the working application states and validates the image files/resolutions. It does **not**, by itself, prove that every crop is the strongest possible marketing/gallery choice.

Before final Devpost upload, visually inspect every image at normal and thumbnail size for:

- legibility;
- accidental clipping;
- excessive empty space;
- distracting transient UI state;
- correct simulation disclosure where relevant;
- whether the proposed order communicates Host quickly;
- whether a tighter crop would improve impact without hiding context.

Do not call the gallery final until that human/visual check has been completed.
